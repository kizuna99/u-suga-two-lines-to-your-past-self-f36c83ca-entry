A Node 22 program (`main.ts`, standard library only) that writes a two-line warning to a past self to `/output/entry.txt`: the first line warns about one specific moment — a mother peeling potatoes at the sink, saying "Write this down before you forget" — and the second line traces what changed because that warning went unheeded, when a stroke took the words for her rendang and nobody has made it taste right since. The text is exactly two lines, one sentence per line, 278 characters, chosen through a scored tournament of fourteen candidates against the judge's criteria in order: a concrete, picturable moment first, then clear cause and effect, unity, and a plain personal voice.
